# Hotel Reservation Operations Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing hotel reservation operations using **SQL and MySQL** to convert reservation data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis helps understand booking demand, guest behaviour, stay performance, staff workload, room utilization, and booking or stay-related issues.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze booking demand across hotels, booking channels, and room types
- Understand guest booking behaviour, booking frequency, and spending patterns
- Evaluate stay duration, outcomes, and stay statuses
- Analyze staff workload and room utilization
- Identify issues such as cancellations, no-shows, and service requests
- Support better operational and data-driven decision-making

## 🗄️ Database Overview

The database consists of six main tables:

| Table | Records | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Guests | 350 | Guest details and preferences |
| Hotels | 25 | Hotel details and room capacity |
| Rooms | 600 | Room types, occupancy, and pricing |
| Bookings | 2,800 | Guest reservation and booking details |
| Stays | 3,200 | Check-in, check-out, stay status, and service details |
| Staff | 70 | Staff details, departments, ratings, and active status |

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project includes SQL-based analysis of:

- Booking demand across hotels, channels, room types, and time periods
- Guest booking frequency, spending, guest types, and booking patterns
- Average, minimum, and maximum stay duration
- Different stay statuses such as Checked-out, In-progress, Cancelled, and No-show
- Staff workload and number of stays handled
- Room usage and operational performance
- Cancellations, no-shows, and service requests

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-demand hotels, booking channels, and room types
- Differences in booking behaviour between Individual and Corporate guests
- Stay duration and stay-status patterns
- Staff workload differences and room utilization
- Areas with higher levels of cancellations, no-shows, and service requests

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project recommends:

- Focusing on higher-performing booking channels
- Maintaining sufficient availability for high-demand room types
- Using guest booking and spending patterns to improve customer engagement
- Monitoring staff workload and room usage
- Tracking cancellations, no-shows, and service requests to improve operations
